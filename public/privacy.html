Byte Acres

Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Byte Acres is a free farming game you play by writing code. We keep as little about you as we can.

On your device

Your farms, code, lessons and settings are also saved in your browser (IndexedDB and localStorage), so the game keeps working when the connection drops. Clearing your browser's site data deletes that copy; the cloud copy stays.

Your account

You play with a free account. It keeps your fields in the cloud, lets you play on another device, and lets you teach or join a class. We store:

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored only as a hash by our sign-in service; nobody can read it. We don't send you email for now.
  • A display name you choose (we start you as "Farmer" and a number).
  • Whether you play on your own, teach or learn.
  • Your fields: the farm, your code, lesson progress and play time.
  • Classes: for teachers, each class's name and join code; for students, the classes you joined and the name you gave there.

Who sees what

  • Your fields and code are visible only to you.
  • In a class, the teacher and the other students see each member's class name, score (what was harvested on the class field), research count, gold and play time on the leaderboard. They do not see your email or your code.
  • The teacher sees who joined and can remove a student from the class.
  • The public leaderboard on the main page shows, to anyone, your display name and one field's name and numbers (score, research, gold, play time). You choose which field in Settings, or switch yourself off the board. Student accounts are off the board unless the student switches it on.

Classes with children

Byte Acres is made to be used in schools. Teachers who use it with students under 16 (or the age of digital consent where they are) should do so with their school's or the parents' permission. A student account needs an email address; a school address is fine. We don't show ads, don't sell data, and don't build profiles.

Fonts are served from this site, not from a font service. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookie. The only things kept in your browser are your saves, your settings and, when signed in, the sign-in session.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. Deleting a field deletes it from the cloud too. A teacher deleting a class deletes every class field in it.

Deleting everything

In the game, open Settings and choose Delete my account. That removes your account, your cloud fields, the classes you teach and your places in classes, straight away. Fields saved only in your browser stay until you delete them or clear site data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used. Write to guykadosh22@gmail.com. If you're in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the game before it takes effect.
